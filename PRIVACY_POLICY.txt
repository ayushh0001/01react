================================================================================
                      PRIVACY POLICY — ZPIN VENDOR DASHBOARD
                        (Seller / Merchant Portal)
================================================================================
Effective Date: March 24, 2026
Last Updated: March 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the ZPIN Vendor Dashboard ("Vendor Portal",
"Platform", "we", "us", or "our"), the seller-facing application that enables
merchants to list products, manage inventory, process orders, and track earnings
on the ZPIN e-commerce ecosystem.

By registering as a seller and using the ZPIN Vendor Dashboard, you agree to
the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. WHO WE ARE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

ZPIN operates a multi-sided e-commerce platform connecting sellers (vendors)
with customers. The Vendor Dashboard is the dedicated portal through which
sellers manage their storefronts, products, and business operations on ZPIN.

For privacy-related queries, contact us at:
  Email  : vendor-privacy@zpin.com
  Address: [Company Address], India

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT FROM SELLERS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

2.1 ACCOUNT AND IDENTITY INFORMATION

  a) Registration Details
     - Full name
     - Email address
     - Mobile number
     - Password (stored in hashed/encrypted form using bcrypt)
     - Username (auto-generated from email prefix)

  b) Personal Profile
     - Date of birth
     - Gender
     - Personal address
     - Personal PAN number (for tax compliance)
     - Profile photograph (optional)

2.2 BUSINESS INFORMATION

  a) Business Details
     - Business name
     - Business description
     - Business type (sole proprietor, partnership, company, etc.)
     - GST number (Goods and Services Tax registration)
     - Business PAN number
     - Business address (street, city, state, pincode)
     - Business verification status

  b) Bank and Financial Details
     - Bank name
     - Bank account number
     - Account holder name
     - Account type (savings / current)
     - IFSC code
     - Bank verification status
     These details are used exclusively for processing seller payouts and
     are stored with encryption. We do not share bank details with customers.

2.3 PRODUCT AND INVENTORY INFORMATION

  a) Product Listings
     - Product name, description, and category
     - Product images (uploaded to secure object storage)
     - Price, discount, and size-based quantities
     - Stock availability status (in stock / out of stock)
     - Product approval status

  b) Category Data
     - Category hierarchy selections (e.g., Fashion > Men > T-Shirts)
     - Size chart associations per category

2.4 ORDER AND TRANSACTION INFORMATION

  a) Order Data
     - Order IDs and order numbers
     - Customer name and delivery address (for fulfillment purposes only)
     - Products ordered, quantities, and prices
     - Order status (pending, processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled)
     - Payment method and payment status

  b) Earnings and Payouts
     - Gross order amount
     - Platform commission / fee deducted
     - Net payout amount
     - Payout history and status
     - Total orders and revenue statistics

2.5 AUTHENTICATION AND SESSION DATA

  a) Login and Session
     - Login timestamps and session duration
     - Authentication tokens (JWT) with 24-hour expiry
     - Session cookies (secure, httpOnly in production)
     - Password reset tokens and timestamps

  b) OAuth Authentication
     - If you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive your name, email, and
       profile picture from Google as permitted by your Google account settings.

2.6 USAGE AND ACTIVITY DATA

  a) Dashboard Interactions
     - Pages visited within the dashboard
     - Products added, edited, or deleted
     - Orders viewed and status updates made
     - Earnings reports accessed

  b) Technical Data
     - IP address
     - Browser type and version
     - Device type and operating system
     - HTTP request logs (method, path, timestamp)

2.7 UPLOADED FILES AND MEDIA

  - Product images uploaded by sellers are stored in our secure object
    storage system (MinIO / S3-compatible storage).
  - Profile photographs are stored separately in a dedicated profile
    image bucket with appropriate access controls.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. HOW WE USE SELLER INFORMATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.1 Account and Identity Verification
     - Verify seller identity before granting access to the platform
     - Conduct KYC (Know Your Customer) checks as required by law
     - Verify GST registration and business legitimacy
     - Verify bank account details before processing payouts

3.2 Platform Operations
     - Enable product listing, editing, and inventory management
     - Process and route customer orders to the correct seller
     - Coordinate with delivery partners for order fulfillment
     - Manage product approval workflows (admin review before listing)
     - Enable real-time order notifications via WebSocket connections

3.3 Financial Processing
     - Calculate seller earnings after platform commission deduction
     - Process payouts to verified bank accounts
     - Generate earnings reports and transaction histories
     - Comply with GST and income tax reporting requirements

3.4 Communication
     - Send order notifications (new orders, status updates, cancellations)
     - Send payout confirmation and earnings summaries
     - Notify sellers of product approval or rejection decisions
     - Send platform updates, policy changes, and feature announcements
     - Respond to seller support queries

3.5 Platform Integrity and Compliance
     - Monitor for fraudulent listings or transactions
     - Enforce seller terms of service
     - Resolve disputes between sellers and customers
     - Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3.6 Analytics and Improvement
     - Analyze seller performance metrics to improve platform tools
     - Identify popular categories and pricing trends
     - Improve the product listing and order management experience

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - Contractual Necessity: Processing required to fulfill our seller
    agreement, including account management, order processing, and payouts.

  - Legal Obligation: GST compliance, income tax reporting, KYC requirements
    under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), and compliance with
    the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

  - Legitimate Interests: Fraud prevention, platform security, analytics,
    and improving seller tools.

  - Consent: Marketing communications and optional features where you have
    given explicit consent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. SHARING SELLER INFORMATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

We do not sell seller data. We share information only as follows:

5.1 With Customers
     - Your business/store name and product information are visible to
       customers on the ZPIN website.
     - Your personal contact details (personal mobile, personal address)
       are NOT shared with customers.

5.2 With Delivery Partners
     - Pickup address and order details are shared with delivery partners
       to enable order collection from your location.

5.3 With the ZPIN Admin Panel
     - Your account details, business information, product listings, orders,
       and earnings are accessible to ZPIN administrators for platform
       management, compliance, and dispute resolution.

5.4 With Payment and Banking Partners
     - Bank account details are shared with our payment processing partners
       solely for the purpose of processing seller payouts.

5.5 With Service Providers
     - Cloud hosting and database providers
     - Object storage providers (for product and profile images)
     - Email and SMS notification providers
     - Analytics and monitoring tools
     All service providers are bound by data processing agreements.

5.6 For Legal Compliance
     - We may disclose seller information to tax authorities (GST, income
       tax), law enforcement, or courts when required by applicable law.

5.7 Business Transfers
     - In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, seller data
       may be transferred to the successor entity.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. SENSITIVE FINANCIAL DATA HANDLING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

We treat bank account details, PAN numbers, and GST numbers as sensitive
financial data. These are:

  - Stored with encryption at rest
  - Accessible only to authorized ZPIN personnel with a legitimate need
  - Never displayed in full in the dashboard UI (masked where shown)
  - Never shared with customers or unauthorized third parties
  - Retained for the period required by Indian financial regulations

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. PRODUCT IMAGES AND MEDIA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Product images uploaded by sellers are:
  - Stored in a secure, access-controlled object storage system
  - Used to display products to customers on the ZPIN website
  - Retained as long as the product listing is active
  - Deleted within 30 days of product deletion upon request

Profile photographs are stored in a separate bucket with public-read access
to enable display within the dashboard and on seller profiles.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. DATA RETENTION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - Account and profile data    : Retained while the seller account is active
                                  and for 3 years after account closure.
  - Business and KYC documents  : Retained for 7 years as required by PMLA
                                  and GST regulations.
  - Order and transaction data  : Retained for 7 years for tax compliance.
  - Earnings and payout records : Retained for 7 years.
  - Product listings            : Retained while active; deleted listings
                                  retained for 1 year for dispute resolution.
  - Session and log data        : Retained for 90 days.
  - Password reset tokens       : Expire after 1 hour and are purged.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. DATA SECURITY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
  - JWT tokens are used for authentication with configurable expiry
  - Session cookies use "secure" and "httpOnly" flags in production
  - Database connections are encrypted
  - Object storage access is controlled via bucket policies
  - Role-based access control limits data access within the platform
  - Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments are conducted
  - Password reset flows use time-limited, single-use tokens

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. YOUR RIGHTS AS A SELLER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable laws:

  a) Right to Access
     Request a copy of the personal and business data we hold about you.

  b) Right to Correction
     Request correction of inaccurate business or personal information.

  c) Right to Erasure
     Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to
     legal retention requirements (financial records, tax compliance).

  d) Right to Data Portability
     Request your product listings, order history, and earnings data in
     a structured, exportable format.

  e) Right to Withdraw Consent
     Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

  f) Right to Grievance Redressal
     Lodge a complaint with our Data Protection Officer or with the
     Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise these rights, contact vendor-privacy@zpin.com. We will respond
within 30 days.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. ACCOUNT SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

If your seller account is suspended or terminated:
  - Active product listings will be unpublished immediately
  - Pending orders will be handled per our seller agreement
  - Financial records will be retained for the legally required period
  - You may request a data export before account closure
  - Personal data not subject to legal retention will be deleted within
    90 days of account closure

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12. COOKIES AND TRACKING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The Vendor Dashboard uses:
  - Session cookies for authentication (required, cannot be disabled)
  - Functional cookies to remember dashboard preferences
  - Analytics cookies to monitor dashboard performance and usage
